# Starting Over

I created a publishing project to get me to write more and since it did not start out with me writing the first files, I feel like it got off the rails. So, I decided to just delete it all and start over. 

I had gotten to the idea of processing content in "themes" that were akin to functions and reminded me of React presentation vs. higher-order components. However, the content was not mine and so it failed to take a shape that I wanted. 

This time, I will keep writing to develop the initial shape. Then, I will allow AI to help me create a site out of the shape I develop. Just like a sculptor molding a ball of clay into a functional pot, I can write out my thoughts and mold those into a compelling site. 

## Boring Site

To not get too out there before grounding myself...and any AI that helps me...I will create the "boring site" of a consulting/publishing site. The goal is to have the typical pages you see on sites and allow visitors to browse the site as they would most everything on the web. 

The main benefit is that this gives us content to use and process into themes...or not, if that does not work out. But we should focus on the vistor and not entertaining ourselves with posibilities that never get anywhere. I have plenty of stalled or failed projects, and I really want this one to succeed. 

So let's treat the boring site like a discovery exercise, shall we...
