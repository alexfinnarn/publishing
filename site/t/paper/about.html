About

I have spent about twelve years in CMS work, mostly Drupal, and most of it on systems that were already running when I got there.

That is the part I actually like. A greenfield build is a design problem. A platform with a decade of decisions in it, real editors depending on it, and money moving through it is a different job — you have to understand why every strange thing is there before you are allowed to remove it.

Most recently I was a Senior Backend Drupal Engineer at CivicActions (2022–2024), working on federal content platforms for the VA and CMS. Before that, the University of Colorado, twice — first on developer tooling and a Vue UI that deployed to more than a thousand sites, then running the online giving platform that moved about $5.4 million a year.

Earlier: Highlights for Children, a Drupal 7 family of sites and the start of their D8 platform. Coplex, where I came in as a contractor and ended up leading the maintenance team across fifteen to twenty clients. Sogeti, on an Ethicon (Johnson & Johnson) Drupal multisite with an offshore team of four.

Along the way: Cypress and Jenkins work as the liaison between development and QA, talks at three regional conferences, accessibility work and a web accessibility certificate from edX, and a habit of writing Architectural Decision Records so the next person does not have to guess.

My degree is in psychology (Ohio State, 2009), which turns out to be more relevant to content modeling than it sounds.

The problems I solve page has the full chronology and the case studies. I also write — mostly CMS first principles and what AI tooling is doing to the shape of this work.
