# Pages (jobs, not routes)

Each line is a job a URL might have later. No paths, no components.

The Site is still for a stranger who might hire. Competence here is direct and
innovative — shown, not explained in a wall of text. The four consultant sites
were useful as a contrast. They are not the bar.

## Required for a competent first version

**Home** — Space. The design leads your eyes around the page. Scrolling can
open a 3D (or other) interactive passage into the rest of the site. First
impression is the job. If they do not want to stay, nothing else matters.
The first response is the ordinary readable web. Interaction is offered, not
required.

**Problem Sets** — A profile of problems I solve. Deeper than a general work
section. Systems thinking: classes of mess, not a grid of past titles. The
shape is still forming.

**Lesson** — A way to learn something and then contact me about it. It should
be based on the problem sets the visitor is interested in. Contact is
oriented, not generic. Hear the idea; do not publish a policy of what I will
not do.

The opening Lesson may be how to begin interacting with the Site (a bounded
component, variants of that component) — only for someone who takes that
Impression. It is not a locked tour in front of Home.

## Impression

Where an interaction starts. Why would someone click, tap, or scroll *this*.
Not a readout after they already acted. Not a catalog of widgets.

Home's job is to offer impressions a stranger might take. Problem Sets and
Lesson are places those interactions can go. Each taken Impression can deepen
the Experience. None of them are mandatory.
