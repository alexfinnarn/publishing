I am beginning a project to guide me towards publishing my thoughts and work. I've been 
publishing content before, but I tend to do it in spurts and then stop. 

For instance, I've redone my personal website I don't know how many times, and it 
usually is around when I don't have a job and am looking for a new one. So, I've tried 
to use the opportunity to grow my skills while at the same time publishing some 
content. 

Arguably, you could say it was a decent idea no matter if I continued writing since I 
needed some kind of project to try new open source libraries and frameworks with. So, 
I would write about setting up the site as a series of tutorials. 

My most successful time was writing on medium.com and getting published in some of the 
periodicals on there. I didn't get much done, but I did have some posts that got tens 
of thousands of views vs my usual tens of views. I was trying to ride the JS wave with 
posts on some of those "whiz bang" frameworks coming out in the 2015 - 2020ish era. 

## Current Stab

I have not been writing since I got my job at DU. Before that I had half-baked 
projects like a 3D three.js shipping container that was supposed to resemble the 
shipping container I bought and was trying to modify. I also had an interesting idea 
to have my personal website like a "choose your own adventure" story and use inkjs for 
that.

Now, I'd rather keep it simple to create a website for professional consulting, like 
mentioned in [Ontology](../ontology.md) as a "Site Surface". I also want to keep the 
other surfaces in mind. 

## To Begin

I can try to write a post a day, almost like a journal entry, and then review my
