# Material for a theme experiment

Only the material section is required. Raw notes or an existing document are
fine; you do not have to fill in a schema or compose a page. A useful first
sample has a clear subject, a few supported details, and a qualification or
limit. Do not invent these just to make the sample fit.

## Material

Paste the material here, or identify the exact files/excerpts to use.
Include source references where available. Separate your observations from
quoted sources when that distinction matters.

## Optional context

- Audience or purpose, if you have one in mind.
- Relationships that must remain true, such as a required sequence.
- Wording that must be quoted exactly, or material that must be included.
- Known uncertainty, missing evidence, or material that is not for publication.

If omitted, the process uses a reader unfamiliar with this particular material
and the purpose “understand the subject, its significance, and its limits.”
Missing source support remains missing; no external research is assumed.

You do not need to supply theme rules, a headline, section names, a lead,
component choices, or a proposed layout. Those are outputs of applying the
supplied policies. Existing headings in your source are context, not mandatory
publication structure unless you mark them as such.
