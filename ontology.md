# Ontology

The point of this file is to keep types from collapsing when they are actually
different. It is not a list of things the Site is forbidden to be.

Types below are *kinds of thing*, not URLs. Questions for writing live in
`questions.md`. Those are not types.

## Surfaces (where something lives)

| Surface | Audience | Job |
|---------|----------|-----|
| **Mick** | me + AI | orientation, paused, weekly. Not public. |
| **Playground** | whoever already knows me | drafts, experiments, informal posts. `~/Sites/personal/content` |
| **Site** | a stranger who might hire, and anyone who should *feel* competence | the public professional surface: space, problem sets, lessons, impressions. Drafted in `site/`. Built as a real website when a page job is clear enough to try. |
| **Books** | a reader who will sit for a sequence | one argument, ordered. Drafted in `books/` |
| **Writebook** | same reader, later | a *publisher* you install on a server. Not a type of writing. See `books/writebook.md` |

Playground is the lab. Site is what the world should see. They can share a
person, essays, and techniques (ink, Three.js, a story, a 3D passage). They
should not share invented proof. If an experiment demonstrates competence, it
can graduate onto the Site. That is the point of a web developer’s consulting
site: show what you can do.

## Content types

**Offer** — what someone pays for, when it exists. Still forming. It does not
have to be a sentence on Home. It can be something they *do* and then
recognize. Without some way to hire, the Site is only a demo. With only a
sentence and no demo, it is a brochure.

**Proof** — named work that happened. CivicActions (VA / CMS), CU giving
platform, Highlights, Ethicon, Coplex support packages. Dates, systems,
outcomes. Not fictional “e-commerce platforms.” Proof can live inside a
problem set or a lesson. It does not need its own wall of case studies.

**Problem set** — a class of problems, not a project title. Systems-shaped.
The visitor should be able to see their mess in it. Still unnamed; do not
fill with résumé bullets to look complete.

**Lesson** — something a visitor learns, tied to the problem sets they showed
interest in, that can turn into a conversation. Not a generic contact page.
Not a Jacobian-style policy of what will not be taken. Hear the idea.

**Impression** — the start of an interaction. Why someone would click, tap, or
scroll *this*. Not a log of what they already did. Analytics, play, and
submissions can come after; they are not the Impression. Not a page.

**Thesis** — how I think about a class of problems. The live one is CMS first
principles (intent before UI, content over complexity, you still succeed if
the CMS fails). A thesis can feed essays, a book, problem sets, and the
offer.

**Essay** — one idea, readable in one sitting. Can stay on the playground, be
mirrored on the Site, become a lesson, or become a book chapter. It is not a
book. The Site should not *rely* on essays as the first impression.

**Series** — essays that already share a prefix and an argument. `CMS First
Principles` is a series. Usual precursor to a book. Not automatically a book.

**Book** — one argument, ordered chapters, a reader starts at 1.

**Chapter** — a unit of a book. May start life as an essay.

**Page** — a Site surface with a job (home, problem sets, lesson). A page is
not an essay and not a service card. It can be interactive.

**Experience** — a way of showing competence that is not a paragraph: space,
motion, a 3D passage, a story branch, a small tool. An Experience can offer
impressions. If it does not make someone want to stay, the text never gets
read.

## Relationships

```
Impression  →  an interaction starts (why this click)
Experience  →  competence shown without a paragraph
Problem Set →  Lesson (learn, then talk)
Lesson      →  conversation / hire
Proof       →  can sit inside a Problem Set or a Lesson
Thesis      →  Series of Essays → Book (if the sequence holds)
Thesis      →  can justify a Problem Set
```

Working through a project in Mick or in a repo is **not** a Book.

## What competence is made of

Competence is being able to do the work, and being able to show it. For this
Site that means:

1. **Direct** — a visitor feels the room and knows they are in the right
   place. Not a wall of text they have to survive.
2. **Innovative** — the Site itself is an example of the craft. Interactive
   where it earns attention. AI is available; use it. Do not wait for a
   finished ontology to try a page.
3. **Problem-shaped** — they can find a mess they have, learn something
   about it, and talk. Proof is checkable when it appears; it is not the
   homepage genre.
4. **Looped** — the Site can change framing from what people actually take
   up, not a one-shot guess. That looking is not the Impression.

The playground `/work` page is still a negative example: service cards, fake
projects, fake testimonials, placeholder Calendly. Do not port *that*. Do
port the ambition to make something someone wants to stay inside.

The hole is not “pick a stack.” It is making Home feel like competence, then
having Problem Sets and a Lesson worth walking into. See `site/pages.md` and
`site/inventory.md`.
